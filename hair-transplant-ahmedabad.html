How it works

A good transplant is mostly planning. The hairline has to suit your face and age, the donor area has to stay full, and your existing hair needs protecting too. That is why every case here starts with a doctor led scalp analysis, not a sales call.

01

Scalp analysis

We check your donor area, hair loss pattern and calibre, then estimate grafts and draw your hairline with you.

02

Extraction

Healthy follicles are taken one by one from the DHT resistant donor zone, within safe limits.

03

Implantation

Each graft is placed at the right angle and direction so the result grows like your own hair.

04

Aftercare

Clear instructions, medicines and follow-ups. PRP or GFC sessions can support graft survival.

Questions

Hair transplant,
answered.

Timelines are typical. Your doctor confirms your exact plan after examining you.

Is a hair transplant permanent?

Yes. Transplanted follicles come from the DHT resistant donor zone, so when the procedure is done correctly they keep growing for life.

Does it hurt?

It is done under local anaesthesia. You stay awake and comfortable, and can watch TV or rest. You feel mild discomfort only during the numbing injection, with some soreness for a day or two.

How long does it take?

Most transplants finish in one day. Around 2000 grafts take 5 to 6 hours. 3000 to 4000 grafts take roughly 7 to 9 hours, decided after a scalp analysis.

When will I see results?

Transplanted hair sheds in 2 to 6 weeks, which is normal. New growth starts around month 3, clear improvement shows by month 6, and the final result arrives at 9 to 12 months.

Who is a good candidate?

Adults above 23 with stable hair loss, a healthy donor area, good general health and realistic expectations. We usually advise against surgery before 23, since baldness may still be progressing.

Will the donor area look thin?

No. When extraction stays within safe limits and is done by an experienced dermatologist, the surrounding hair covers the donor zone naturally.

When can I go back to work and the gym?

Desk work in 2 to 3 days, physical work in 7 to 10 days. Light walking is fine after 3 to 4 days, but avoid sweating, swimming and heavy workouts for 2 to 3 weeks.

Will a transplant stop future hair loss?

It restores lost hair but does not stop thinning of your native hair. We protect existing density with medicines such as minoxidil, finasteride for suitable men, and often PRP or GFC.
